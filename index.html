About

I like knowing what happens between writing code and a service actually running.

I'm a recent B.Tech graduate in Computer Science and Engineering, currently training as a DevOps trainee at Zoople Technologies. My focus is the practical side: Linux, containers, deployment workflows and the cloud.

I learn by building. My projects so far include a three-tier application deployed with Docker and Kubernetes, and a smart card web application for a college bus system. I also work with the fundamentals of IT support: Windows troubleshooting, Microsoft 365 administration and account management.

I'm early in my career and looking for a team where I can keep learning while helping keep systems reliable.
